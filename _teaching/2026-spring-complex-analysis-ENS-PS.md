Course Overview
======

Teaching Assistant / Instructor for exercise sessions (*Travaux Dirigés*) in **Complex Analysis (L3 level)** at ENS Paris-Saclay. 


Course Syllabus & Topics
======

* **Holomorphic Functions:** Cauchy-Riemann equations, power series, and harmonic conjugates.
* **Cauchy Theory:** Cauchy integral formulas, Liouville's theorem, and maximum modulus principle.
* **Singularities & Residues:** Laurent series, residue theorem, and evaluation of real integrals.
