Executive Summary
======

In this expository note, we discuss the asymptotic behavior of kink solutions for non-linear wave equations and Yang-Mills fields in black hole backgrounds.

Main Setup
======

Consider the scalar field equation on a static background metric $g_{\mu\nu}$:

$$\Box_g \phi + V'(\phi) = 0$$

where $V(\phi) = \frac{1}{4}(\phi^2 - 1)^2$ denotes the standard double-well potential.

Key Analytical Techniques
------

1. **Virial Estimates:** Used to control localized energy decay.
2. **Spectral Analysis:** Understanding the linear stability operator $L = -\Delta + V''(\phi_0)$.
