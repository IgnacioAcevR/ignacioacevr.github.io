Parc de Sceaux — A place to escape in Île-de-France
======

Located midway along the line between Orsay and central Paris, the **Parc de Sceaux** has become one of my favorite places for outdoor activities. It is the perfect spot to run, walk, read a book, or enjoy a coffee. 

The vast expanse of the park allows visitors to appreciate the formal French garden style (*jardin à la française*) without the heavy crowds typical of other notable sites like the *Jardin du Luxembourg* or the gardens of *Versailles*.
