Research School: Mathematical Developments in Geophysical Fluid Dynamics
======

Held at the **Centre International de Rencontres Mathématiques (CIRM)** in Luminy (Marseille), this research school focused on introductory and advanced courses exploring PDEs in fluid mechanics alongside the physics of geophysical flows. The primary objective was to build analytical tools and foster interdisciplinary collaboration between the mathematical and geophysical research communities.

Featured Mini-Courses
======

* **Anne-Laure Dalibard** *(Sorbonne Université)*  
  *Mathematical methods for the analysis of multi-scale problems in the ocean.*
* **Rupert Klein** *(Freie Universität Berlin)*  
  *Multiscale modelling for the atmosphere.*
